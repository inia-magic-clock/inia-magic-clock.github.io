Magic Clock Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

Magic Clock ("the app", package name com.inia.magicclock.free) is developed by inia ("we", "us") and distributed on Google Play.

Contact: inia.devel@gmail.com

1. Summary

Magic Clock is a focus/break timer that runs entirely on your device. We do not require an account and we never see your sessions, streaks or settings — they are stored only on your phone. The app shows Google AdMob advertisements and sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics, as described in Sections 3 and 5. Extreme mode uses a VPN that never leaves your device (Section 2).

2. Permissions the app uses, and why

  • Foreground service — keeps the timer accurate while the app is in the background.
  • Post notifications — shows the running timer in the notification shade and on the lock screen.
  • Internet and advertising ID — required by Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics.
  • VPN service (extreme mode only) — extreme mode, an optional Magic Clock Pro feature, turns the internet off during focus time. It does this with Android's VPN service, used entirely on your device: while focus time runs, the app takes all network traffic and drops it. No traffic is read, logged, stored, or sent to any server, ours or anyone else's. The VPN is only active during focus time, only after you switch extreme mode on and accept Android's VPN dialog, and it goes off for breaks, pauses and when the session ends.

3. Advertising

The app shows a banner ad while a session is running, during both focus and break. No ads are shown on the start screen or the session summary, and none at all once Magic Clock Pro is bought.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, general device information, diagnostics about how ads load and perform, and approximate location. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form before any ad is requested; declining costs nothing — ads become non-personalised or are not shown, and the app is otherwise unchanged. You can change your choice at any time in Android's Settings → Google → Ads. AdMob's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases

Magic Clock Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can unlock the Pro features on your devices. Google's handling of payments is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Usage statistics

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand whether the app is used and whether it works. It reports the following events:

  • that the app was installed, opened, updated or removed;
  • that a session was completed, with its length in minutes of focus and total time;
  • the app version, Android version, device model, language and country.

Events are attached to a random identifier Firebase creates for the installation and, where you have allowed it, to your advertising ID. We look at this data only in aggregate — how many people use the app, how often, for how long — and never use it to identify or contact anyone. Google processes it under the Firebase privacy and security terms; per-user data is retained for up to 14 months.

We have no account system, no crash reporting and no servers of our own. Your session history, daily focus totals, streaks and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

6. Your choices

You can decline personalised ads in the consent form, or reset your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Google → Ads. If you have a question about data, write to the address in Section 9.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. No child-directed ad treatment is configured.

8. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page changes first, and the effective date above with it. Please check back occasionally.

9. Contact

Questions may be directed to inia.devel@gmail.com.
